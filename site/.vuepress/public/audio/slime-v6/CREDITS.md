# Slime kit v6

Clay sounds retain v5 qubodup CC0 excerpts and normalization:
https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/sounds/127449/
Pop sounds retain the accepted rubberduck9999 CC0 excerpts:
https://freesound.org/people/rubberduck9999/sounds/680446/
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The four glitter/foil WAVs are restored byte-for-byte from the project's v3
kit, originally generated by `scripts/gen-slime-sounds.mjs` (project-owned
glass-like glitter chimes and lower metallic foil chimes, not CC0 recordings).
Sprinkling plays these without the clay loop or clay release sound.

Source hashes and sample provenance are in manifest.json. Rebuild using:
`node scripts/prepare-slime-recordings.mjs /path/to/sources`
See v5/CREDITS.md for the recording processing and source download details.
Interface actions and camera movement do not trigger audible samples.
