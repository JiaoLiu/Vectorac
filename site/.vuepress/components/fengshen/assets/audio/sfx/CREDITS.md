# 众神斗法 audio credits

Background music: “Samurai Nights” by Maik Hoffmann (Majadroid).
Erhu/dizi/qin traditional Chinese instruments, composed for battle tension.

Official asset page: https://opengameart.org/content/samurai-nights
Author page: https://opengameart.org/users/majadroid
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
(Asset page also offers CC BY 3.0 and OGA-BY 3.0; CC BY 4.0 was chosen.)

The full demo track is used as the looping background music (file: heavenly-duel.mp3).
It is played at low volume (0.15) with `loop=true`; the source track's quiet intro and
outro make the loop seam unobtrusive.
